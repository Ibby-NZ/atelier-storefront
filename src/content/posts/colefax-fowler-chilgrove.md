Colefax & Fowler is the house that gave the world English Country House style: faded chintz beside period silk, humble pieces alongside the grand, rooms that feel collected rather than decorated. It is a look built on ease, and it has always borrowed freely.

The Chilgrove collection borrows further afield than most. Richly layered, globally inspired prints, embroideries, weaves and plains draw on Persian and Suzani patchwork traditions. Tamarind and Perseus are the two designs to start with, both carrying the slightly weathered palette the house is known for.

What keeps it English is the restraint. Colour is muted rather than saturated, pattern is scaled to live with, and every print has a plain and a stripe to sit beside it, so a scheme can be built from a single collection without ever feeling matched.

Chilgrove is available by the metre for drapery and upholstery. Samples can be requested from any colourway on its product page, and the full hangers are in the trade showrooms.
