Linen, wool, cashmere, hemp and silk are the fibres our houses are built on. De Le Cuona sources flax, hemp, wool, cashmere, alpaca and silk from the world's finest growers, and Loro Piana has pursued exceptional natural fibres since 1924. Fabrics like these age beautifully, but they reward a little care.

Light is the first thing to manage. Direct sun fades any dye over time and weakens silk in particular, so line your drapery, and where a chair sits in a sunny window, turn the cushions regularly so the fading is even rather than striped.

Spills should be blotted, never rubbed, with a clean white cloth, working from the edge of the mark inward. Most natural fibre upholstery is best cleaned professionally, and the care label tells the cleaner what the fabric will tolerate.

Regular vacuuming with an upholstery attachment keeps grit from cutting the fibres at the seams, and a throw laid across the seat of a much-used sofa can be washed or aired far more easily than the upholstery beneath it. Loro Piana's throws and blankets were made for exactly this.

When in doubt, ask. The showroom team can tell you how a particular cloth has behaved in other homes, and a cutting kept from your original order is the best possible reference for any future cleaning or repair.
