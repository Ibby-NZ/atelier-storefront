No photograph tells you how a fabric feels in the hand or how its colour shifts as the light moves across a room. A cutting does, and requesting one is the best first step in any scheme.

Every colourway on the site has a Request a sample action on its product page. Ask for the ones you are considering and tell us where they are going, and we will post cuttings from our sample library. If you have an account, each cutting is recorded against it, so the samples and the eventual order stay together.

Look at a cutting where the fabric will live, at the times of day the room is used. Hold it vertically for drapery and flat for upholstery, because a weave can read quite differently in each. Pin it beside the paint, the timber and the other fabrics rather than judging it alone.

For larger decisions the showrooms hold full-width hangers and, for trade clients, loan samples that can leave the building for a site visit. Appointments are Monday to Friday in Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch.

When you are ready, order by the metre online or through the showroom, and keep a cutting from the delivered roll. It is the reference every future cleaning, repair or addition will want.
