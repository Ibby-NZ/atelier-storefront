Cappuccino is Pierre Frey at its most quietly confident: warm neutrals, subtle
contrasts and striking plays of light across prints and weaves. This season the
house pairs free-spirited motifs — anemones, toucans, kiwis — with timeless
grounds that sit comfortably in both classic and contemporary interiors.

The collection is available by the metre. Browse the Pierre Frey range and
request a cutting to see the hand and colour in person.
