Jack Lenor Larsen founded his New York textile house in 1952 believing textiles could be as expressive as art. A trained weaver with a passion for global craft traditions, he brought ikat, batik and handweaving into modern interiors, and that appetite for the world's making is still the house's compass.

The Larsen 2024 collection takes African motifs as its starting point, paying homage to traditional pattern while translating it into the house's own vocabulary of texture and natural fibre. Rhythmic geometry sits beside soft plains, and the weaves keep the irregularity of the hand even where the loom is modern.

Woven wallcoverings are where Larsen is at its most distinctive. Grasscloth, raffia and linen bring depth and a connection to nature to a wall, and this season's palette of ochre, bark and bone reads as warmly in a city apartment as in a coastal house.

Larsen is part of the Colefax Group, and the collection is in the Auckland and Wellington showrooms now. Browse the range online and request a sample to see the weave in your own light.
