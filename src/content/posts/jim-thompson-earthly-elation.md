In 1948 an American architect arrived in Bangkok and became captivated by a fading craft: the hand-woven silk of Ban Krua. Jim Thompson helped revive Thai silk, refined its production and brought it to the world's interiors, and his disappearance in 1967 only added to the legend of the house that carries his name.

Earthly Elation is the house looking to the ground rather than the palace. Nature's raw aesthetic is rendered in rock, bark, moss and leaf tones, and plaits, plains, stripes and checks are reimagined in shifting scales, so a small check and a broad plait share a colour story without repeating each other.

The silk is still there, in the lustre of the plains and the depth the dyes achieve, but the collection is as much about weave as shine. It is a quieter Jim Thompson, well suited to rooms that want texture more than pattern.
