A note on what has arrived at Atelier this autumn, what the showrooms are showing, and where to find us.

Pierre Frey's Belvedere is the collection people keep asking to see. A high-performance indoor and outdoor range, it blends graphic compositions at a large, spectacular scale, with flora, fauna and dynamic geometrics in a palette that stands up to a New Zealand summer as well as it stands up in a room. The hangers are in all three showrooms.

Houlès has refreshed its curtain hardware, and the Tantulo collection of poles, finials and fittings is now on display in Auckland alongside the passementerie. Trimmings are the finishing touch most often left to the end of a project. This is a reminder to bring them in at the start.

Liberty London's latest fabrics and wallcoverings reach back into the Regent Street archive for botanical motifs and painterly florals. The Tana Lawn cottons are joined by heavier weights for upholstery, so a Liberty print no longer has to be reserved for a cushion.

Trade showrooms in Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch are open Monday to Friday by appointment. Browse the full catalogue online, and request samples from any product page.
