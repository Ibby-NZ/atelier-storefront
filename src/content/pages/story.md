---
title: Our Story
---

Atelier Textiles is a New Zealand agency representing some of the world's most
distinctive fabric and wallcovering houses. For over two decades we have brought
European craftsmanship to interior designers, architects and homeowners across
Aotearoa — pairing an inventive, deeply eclectic catalogue with the local service
of a small, dedicated team.

We work by the metre and by relationship. Every fabric we carry is chosen for its
hand, its colour and the story of the house behind it, from three-generation
French family businesses to contemporary performance-cloth innovators. When you
request a cutting or place an order, a real person confirms stock, batch shading
and cut requirements before anything is invoiced.

Our showroom holds the full range of samples, and our journal follows the
collections as they arrive each season. Whether you are specifying for a single
room or an entire project, we would love to help you find the right cloth.
