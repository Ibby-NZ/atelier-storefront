title: Contact
---

We would love to hear about your project. Reach the Atelier Textiles team for
samples, trade enquiries, stock checks and cut requirements — a real person
responds to every enquiry, usually within one business day.

Trade customers can [apply for a trade account](/trade-application) to access
trade pricing across the catalogue, or browse the [product library](/products)
to request a cutting from any fabric page. For an existing order, please include
your order number so we can help you quickly.
