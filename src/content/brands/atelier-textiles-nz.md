It’s a rare combination — Old World weaving tradition paired with New World
fibre at its finest. Every colourway is drawn from the natural world itself: the
tones of stone, earth, foliage and coastline that surround us, translated into a
palette that feels inherently right in a New Zealand home. This is luxury
textile made on our own terms — refined, considered, and genuinely ours from
concept to cloth.
