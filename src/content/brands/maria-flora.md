Known as the Rolls Royce of outdoor fabrics, Mariaflora has redefined
performance textiles with its signature linen-look technology; creating soft,
natural-feeling fabrics that deliver exceptional resistance to stains, fading
and weather. Collections such as Erfoud and Zagora capture the relaxed elegance
of linen, while luxurious velvets bring the same refined sensibility indoors.

Particularly suited to New Zealand’s strong UV light, Mariaflora fabrics offer
exceptional colour stability, durability and easy care without compromising on
beauty. The result is effortless Italian living; fabrics that combine the
tactile richness of luxury interiors with the resilience required for modern
homes, indoors, outdoors and beyond.
