blurb: >-
  The story of C&C Milano begins in 1850, when Clateo Castellini took over a
  small hemp and wool spinning mill outside Milan, laying the foundations for
  generations of Italian textile craftsmanship. In 1996, cousins Piero
  Castellini Baldissera and Emanuele Castellini combined the family’s textile
  heritage with a passion for timeless design to create C&C Milano.
---

Today, the house is renowned for indoor and outdoor fabrics, wallcoverings,
trimmings, and bespoke bed, bath and table linen, all celebrating the beauty of
natural fibres, particularly linen. Defined by sun-bleached tones, relaxed
textures and a distinctly Milanese sense of understated elegance, every fabric
is finished and packaged by hand in the company’s own workshop; a level of care
that reflects true Italian craftsmanship.

The result is effortless luxury; warm, tactile and quietly refined, embodying
the spirit of la dolce vita.
