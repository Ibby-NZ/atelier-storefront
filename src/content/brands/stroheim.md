blurb: >-
  Founded in New York in 1860, Stroheim is one of America’s most enduring
  textile houses, built on a tradition of craftsmanship, innovation and refined
  design. Originally known for importing European textiles, the company evolved
  into a celebrated source of decorative fabrics, combining historic influences
  with a distinctly American sense of style.
---

Acquired by Fabricut in 2014, Stroheim continues to be recognised for its
sophisticated mix of timeless patterns, luxurious textures, rich velvets,
elegant linens and beautifully woven textiles. Drawing from its extensive
archives while embracing contemporary colour and technique, Stroheim creates
fabrics with depth and character; refined, versatile and designed for interiors
that feel both classic and current.
