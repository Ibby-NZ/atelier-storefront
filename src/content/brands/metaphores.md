Inspired by nature, art and architecture, Metaphores creates richly textured
bouclés, elegant linens, sophisticated jacquards, velvets, expressive woven
designs and tactile wallcoverings. Each collection celebrates the beauty of the
material itself; refined, layered and timeless.

The result is distinctly French design with quiet sophistication; a balance of
heritage craftsmanship, contemporary creativity and understated elegance.
