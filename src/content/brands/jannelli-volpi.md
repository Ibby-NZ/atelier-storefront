  Milan, 13 May 1961. Oreste Jannelli, alongside his wife and brother-in-law,
  founded a wallpaper company on the conviction that Italy could lead the world
  in a craft still dominated by others. Sixty years on — the milestone
  anniversary celebrated in 2021 — the house remains entirely family-run, now
  led by Oreste’s children Mauro, Paola and Lidia, and still keeps the very
  first loom from 1930 on display at its headquarters, a living link to where it
  all began.
---

From a state-of-the-art plant outside Milan, Jannelli & Volpi now produces
16,500 kilos of wallpaper daily, entirely Made in Italy, spanning collections
drawn from Japanese iconography, historic Italian palazzi and distant landscapes
alike — alongside prestigious licensed collections for Armani/Casa and Missoni.

This is wallcovering with genuine Italian authority: technically masterful,
endlessly inventive, and rooted in six decades of one family’s unbroken craft.
