blurb: >-
  Garrett Leather began in 1988 when founder Cameron Brown launched the company
  from the attic of his own home; a “garret” in Old English; with a singular
  focus on premium leather for private aircraft. From those beginnings, the
  family-owned company has become a global leader in luxury leather, trusted for
  private jets, superyachts, hospitality and the world’s finest residential
  interiors.
---

Sourcing only the highest-quality European hides, Garrett Leather is renowned
for exceptional craftsmanship, rich colour and innovative finishes. Alongside
its extensive collection, the company offers bespoke services including custom
colour matching, embossing, laser etching, perforating, quilting and finishing;
enabling designers to create truly individual interiors.

The result is leather at its finest; natural, tactile and timeless, crafted to
develop character with age and tailored to the exacting standards of the world’s
leading designers.
