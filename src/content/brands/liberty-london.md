blurb: >-
  Arthur Lasenby Liberty opened his famous Regent Street emporium in London in
  1875 with a vision of bringing the beauty of the wider world to Britain.
  Inspired by the arts and crafts of Japan, India and the East, Liberty
  introduced fabrics and objects that helped shape the Aesthetic and Art Nouveau
  movements.
---

Today, Liberty continues to draw from its vast archive across indoor and outdoor
fabrics and wallcoverings, reimagining botanical motifs, painterly florals and
intricate patterns for contemporary interiors. Best known for its fine cottons,
including the iconic Tana Lawn™ cloth, Liberty creates designs that are joyful
yet refined; richly detailed, timeless and unmistakably British.
