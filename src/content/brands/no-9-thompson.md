  No.9 Thompson brings a relaxed, contemporary spirit to the heritage of Jim
  Thompson, the legendary Thai silk house. Created by British designer Richard
  Smith, founder and creative force behind Madeaux, the collection reflects his
  distinctive approach to colour, pattern and craftsmanship.

Drawing on his passion for hand-painted design, travel and historic textiles,
Richard Smith creates expressive prints, elegant weaves and a more informal
sense of decoration across fabrics, outdoor textiles and wallcoverings. Produced
through the expertise of the Jim Thompson mill in Thailand, the collection
combines exceptional craftsmanship with a fresh, modern sensibility.

The result is a collection that feels worldly yet approachable; rich in colour,
full of character and infused with the effortless elegance that defines Richard
Smith’s signature.
