blurb: >-
  Dominique Kieffer founded her Paris textile house in 1972 with a belief that
  the finest fabrics should speak through texture rather than ornament. Inspired
  by French refinement and Italian craftsmanship, she created collections that
  celebrated the natural beauty of fibres through tactile weaves, nuanced colour
  and exceptional materials. In 2001, the house joined the Rubelli Group, where
  its distinctive design philosophy continues to evolve.
---

Today, Kieffer remains focused on materiality, innovation and contemporary
elegance. Woven in Italy by specialist mills, the collections explore luxurious
linens, sculptural textures, refined sheers and expressive new materials. With a
modern, understated approach to design, Kieffer creates fabrics that feel
effortlessly sophisticated; timeless yet contemporary, restrained yet rich in
character.
