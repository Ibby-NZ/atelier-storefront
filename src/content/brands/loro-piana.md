  Since 1924, when Pietro Loro Piana formalised generations of family expertise
  into a single Italian mill, Loro Piana has pursued an enduring obsession: the
  world’s most exceptional natural fibres. Best known for its luxurious
  cashmere, the house is also renowned for rare materials including vicuña from
  the wild highlands of the Andes, baby cashmere from Mongolian Hircus goats and
  Gift of Kings® merino from a private strain of sheep raised across New Zealand
  and Australia.
---

Launched in 2006, Loro Piana Interiors brings this heritage into private homes,
yachts and residences through exquisite indoor and outdoor fabrics,
wallcoverings, throws, blankets and area rugs. Inspired by the landscapes from
which these fibres originate, the collections are defined by quiet natural
palettes, extraordinary softness and uncompromising craftsmanship. Through its
bespoke Made to Measure service, each creation can be tailored to individual
projects, offering personalised solutions in fabric, colour and finish.

Loro Piana Interiors represents the pinnacle of Italian textile excellence;
creations that transcend trend and become treasured expressions of comfort,
refinement and timeless beauty.
