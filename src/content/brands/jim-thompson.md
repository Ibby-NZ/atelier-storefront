blurb: >-
  In 1948, American architect and wartime intelligence officer Jim Thompson
  arrived in Bangkok and became captivated by a fading craft; the hand-woven
  silk produced on village looms in Ban Krua. Rather than simply collect it, he
  helped revive Thai silk, refining production, introducing vivid new dyes and
  bringing this centuries-old tradition to the world’s interiors and fashion
  houses. His mysterious disappearance in 1967 only added to the enduring legend
  of the brand.
---

Today, Jim Thompson continues to celebrate this heritage through luxurious
fabrics and wallcoverings. Drawing on the colours, textures and patterns of
Southeast Asia, the collections are rich with silk, intricate weaving and bold
design. The result is textiles with unmistakable character; lustrous, expressive
and deeply rooted in the artistry of Thai craftsmanship.
