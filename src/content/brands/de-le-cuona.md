blurb: >-
  Founded in 1992 by Bernie de Le Cuona, de Le Cuona is built on a singular
  devotion to raw natural fibres, transformed through exceptional craftsmanship
  into rare and refined textiles. Flax, hemp, wool, cashmere, alpaca and silk
  are sourced from the world’s finest growers, then shaped by master weavers and
  hand finishers using techniques such as stonewashing, embossing and
  traditional weaving.
---

Colour is drawn from the natural world; landscape, mineral, spice and season
translated into nuanced palettes developed through meticulous sampling.
Alongside its collections, de Le Cuona offers a bespoke service, creating
customised textiles and tailored solutions for projects requiring a truly
individual expression.

The result is fabric of understated elegance and extraordinary depth; quietly
luxurious, richly tactile and carrying the unmistakable story of the
craftsmanship behind every piece.
