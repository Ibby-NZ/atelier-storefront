blurb: >-
  In 1906, Sol Harris founded his own boutique textile house in New York,
  quickly making a name for himself with lavish embroideries, fashion-forward
  jacquards and spellbinding prints — a designer whose ostentatious eye made him
  an arbiter of style well before “trend forecasting” existed as a job. In 1988,
  S. Harris joined the Fabricut family, gaining the reach of a global supplier
  while keeping the boutique sensibility that had defined it for eighty years.
---

Since 2015, the house has been led by Jodi Finer, who brings both a fashion
background and a personal connection to its legacy — her grandfather founded
Fabricut itself. Under her direction, S. Harris has evolved into a genuinely
socially conscious brand, partnering with nonprofits while continuing Sol’s
original instinct for the bold and the beautiful.

This is fabric with real personality: fashion-literate, artistically ambitious,
and led today by someone building a legacy of her own.
