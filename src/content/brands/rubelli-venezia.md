  Founded in Venice in 1858 by Lorenzo Rubelli, the Rubelli textile house was
  born from a passion for preserving and reinterpreting the splendour of
  historic Venetian weaving traditions. For more than 160 years, the family-run
  company has remained devoted to exceptional craftsmanship, creating fabrics
  and wallcoverings that combine heritage techniques with contemporary design.
---

Drawing inspiration from art, architecture and its Venetian roots, Rubelli is
renowned for rich and lustrous silks, sumptuous velvets, intricate damasks,
elegant jacquards, innovative woven textiles and wallcoverings. With its own
historic archives and weaving mill in Como, the house balances centuries of
textile knowledge with a modern design sensibility; creating interiors of
extraordinary depth, character and unmistakable Italian elegance.
