blurb: >-
  Founded in Paris in 1963, Manuel Canovas created his namesake textile house
  with a passion for colour, travel and the decorative arts. Trained at the
  École du Louvre and the École des Beaux-Arts, he brought a painter’s eye and
  an archaeologist’s curiosity to design, transforming traditional motifs such
  as toiles de Jouy, florals and exotic patterns through unexpected colour,
  generous scale and a distinctly French sense of joie de vivre.
---

Today, Manuel Canovas continues this spirit across fabrics, wallcoverings and
trimmings. Known for vibrant prints, elegant embroideries, richly coloured
linens, velvets and woven textiles, the house blends 18th-century French
elegance with a more adventurous, worldly sensibility. Acquired by the Colefax
Group in 1998, it remains one of the most expressive voices in decorative
textiles; romantic yet fresh, sophisticated yet full of life.
