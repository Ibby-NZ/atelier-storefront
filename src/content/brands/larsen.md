blurb: >-
  Jack Lenor Larsen founded his New York textile house in 1952 with the belief
  that textiles could be as expressive as art. A trained weaver with a passion
  for global craft traditions, he introduced a new language of modern interiors
  through innovative weaving, rich textures and natural fibres, transforming
  techniques such as ikat, batik and handweaving into sophisticated contemporary
  designs.
---

Today, Larsen continues to honour the artistry of materials as part of the
Colefax Group, extending its vision across fabrics and wallcoverings. The
collection includes tactile woven wallcoverings crafted from natural fibres such
as grasscloth, raffia, linen and other organic materials, bringing depth,
texture and a connection to nature into interiors.

Combined with refined textiles, layered surfaces and organic forms, Larsen
creates spaces that balance craftsmanship, creativity and quiet sophistication.
