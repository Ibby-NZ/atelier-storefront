blurb: >-
  Marcel Boussac arrived in Paris in 1907 with almost nothing and built France’s
  largest textile empire, earning the title “France’s Cotton King.” In 1934, he
  acquired the historic Wesserling print works in Alsace, introducing new
  printing techniques to create the bold, colourful florals that became his
  signature. His influence extended beyond textiles; in 1946, he financed the
  launch of a young Christian Dior, beginning a lifelong friendship.
---

Today, under the custodianship of Pierre Frey, Boussac continues to celebrate
its heritage through vivid florals, graphic prints and confident colour. With an
archive of over 1,500 historic designs, the house remains a symbol of French
textile creativity, craftsmanship and bold decorative expression.
