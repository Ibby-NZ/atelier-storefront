blurb: >-
  Founded by renowned British designer Mary Fox Linton, whose interiors were
  celebrated for their quiet elegance, layered textures and masterful use of
  natural materials, Fox Linton reflects her belief that the finest luxury is
  understated. Working with specialist mills across Britain and Europe, the
  house creates beautifully crafted indoor and outdoor fabrics and throws using
  exceptional fibres including wool, linen, silk and velvet. The signature is a
  timeless palette and a focus on weave, texture and the inherent beauty of the
  material itself.
---

Guided by Fox Linton’s philosophy of craftsmanship, authenticity and enduring
design, every collection is created to complement architecture rather than
compete with it. The result is fabric that is tactile, quietly luxurious and
effortlessly elegant; designed to be lived with and appreciated for generations.
