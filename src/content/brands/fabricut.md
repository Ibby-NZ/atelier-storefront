blurb: >-
  Two friends, Joe Finer and Harry Guterman, survived Nazi Germany and reunited
  in postwar New York, where they ran a small drapery store with a shared dream:
  to create a wholesale fabric house of their own. In 1954, they brought that
  vision to Tulsa, Oklahoma, transforming an unlikely location into the
  foundation of one of America’s largest textile companies. Still family-run
  three generations later, Fabricut has remained partly employee-owned since
  1998.
---

Today, Fabricut offers an exceptional breadth of fabrics, wallcoverings and a
wide range of trimmings; spanning contemporary to classic design. Alongside its
own collections, the house continues to steward renowned names including
Clarence House, S Harris, Vervain and Stroheim. Its legacy remains rooted in a
distinctly American story of resilience, craftsmanship and reinvention.
