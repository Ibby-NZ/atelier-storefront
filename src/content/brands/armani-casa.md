blurb: >-
  Launched in 2014 through a partnership with the Italian wallcovering
  specialists Jannelli & Volpi, the Armani Casa wallcovering collection extends
  Giorgio Armani’s design philosophy onto the wall itself; restraint, proportion
  and an obsession with material honesty applied at architectural scale.
---

“I have a taste for the most precious materials,” Armani has said, “enhanced by
pure lines, exquisite craftsmanship and natural, harmonious colours”. This
translates into surfaces with real depth: tonal jacquard-effect weaves, subtle
metallic sheens and a palette of stone, bronze and ink drawn straight from the
Armani wardrobe.

These are wallcoverings built to be felt as much as seen: texture over pattern,
atmosphere over statement. Considered, quiet, and unmistakably Milanese.
