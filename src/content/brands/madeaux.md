blurb: >-
  After more than 30 years designing for leading fabric houses, including Jim
  Thompson’s No.9 brand, Richard Smith created Madeaux in East Sussex in 2011
  with a vision to bring a fresh perspective to traditional English decoration.
  Inspired by nature, travel, art and antique textiles, his designs combine
  heritage, craftsmanship and an effortless sense of ease.
---

Beginning as hand-painted watercolours, Madeaux collections evolve into richly
detailed prints, embroideries, woven fabrics and wallcoverings, alongside
outdoor textiles that extend the same decorative spirit beyond the home. With
timeless florals, elegant stripes and tactile textures, the house creates
designs that feel collected over time; traditional yet relaxed, refined yet full
of character.
