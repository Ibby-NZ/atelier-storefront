Pierre Frey designs, weaves and prints its own fabrics, wallpapers and rugs in
France, drawing on an archive of more than 25,000 documents. Every season the
house balances heritage craftsmanship with a playful, contemporary eye — from
hand-drawn florals to bold indoor–outdoor performance cloth.

We stock Pierre Frey by the metre for trade and retail. Request a cutting or an
order through any product page, and our team will confirm stock, batch shading
and cut requirements before invoicing.
