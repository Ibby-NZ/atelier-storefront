Today, Maison Pierre Frey creates fabrics, wallcoverings, furniture and area
rugs that celebrate colour, craftsmanship and storytelling. With its own French
workshops, a commitment to preserving rare artisanal skills and an archive
spanning centuries of decorative arts, the house continues to honour traditional
techniques while embracing contemporary creativity.

Each collection is a dialogue between history and imagination; from silk, wool,
velvet and hand-dyed ikats inspired by historic textiles, global cultures and
contemporary art to bold, joyful designs that feel distinctly Parisian. The
result is decoration with true esprit; layered, expressive and confident, where
heritage is preserved while constantly being reinvented.
