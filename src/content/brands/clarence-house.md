blurb: >-
  Named after the Queen Mother’s London residence, Clarence House was founded in
  New York in 1961 by interior designer Robin Roberts to introduce exceptional
  European textiles to the American market. By the 1970s, the house had evolved
  into a design studio in its own right, sourcing from master mills across
  Europe and Asia and creating collections distinguished by artistry,
  craftsmanship and bold originality.
---

Today, owned by the Fabricut Group, Clarence House is renowned for luxurious
fabrics and wallcoverings that embrace fearless colour, rich texture and
decorative flair. Under the long-standing creative direction of Kazumi Yoshida,
the house has produced iconic designs such as the celebrated Tibet Tiger,
alongside hand screen-printed fabrics, sumptuous silks, dramatic velvets and
expressive wallpapers.

The result is a collection with unmistakable personality; glamorous, artistic
and endlessly inspiring for the world’s leading designers.
