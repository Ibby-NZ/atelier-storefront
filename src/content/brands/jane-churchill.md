blurb: >-
  In London in the 1980s, English decoration was evolving. Formal country-house
  interiors were giving way to a lighter, more relaxed way of living, and
  designer Jane Churchill arrived at precisely the right moment. Founded in
  1982, the brand reimagined British decorating tradition for a new generation,
  introducing fresh colour, playful pattern and effortless elegance across
  textiles, wallcoverings and outdoor fabrics. Just seven years later, it joined
  the Colefax Group, where its contemporary spirit complemented the quiet
  classicism of Colefax & Fowler.
---

That spirit continues throughout every collection. Painterly florals, crisp
geometrics, lively embroideries, textured weaves, decorative wallpapers and
performance outdoor fabrics bring colour and character without overwhelming a
room. The aesthetic is unmistakably British, but with a lighter touch;
sophisticated without stiffness, cheerful without excess and designed for homes
that feel lived in rather than decorated.
