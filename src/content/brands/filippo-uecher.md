blurb: >-
  In Biella — the Italian valley whose textile roots run back to the
  pre-Romanesque period — a fourth-generation weaver named Filippo Uecher spent
  thirty years perfecting his craft as a quiet subcontractor to some of the
  biggest names in fabric. In 2015, he finally put his own name on the door,
  founding a house built entirely around noble natural fibres: wool, linen,
  cashmere, alpaca, woven exclusively by local Biella artisans he’s known his
  whole career.
---

“My whole life is woven into this collection,” he says of the work — and it
shows. Every piece draws on personal history: his travels, his relationships,
his family’s textile legacy, translated into fabrics of extraordinary softness
and quiet refinement.

This is luxury on an intimate, human scale — small-batch, deeply personal, and
made by the hands of someone who has genuinely given his life to the loom.
