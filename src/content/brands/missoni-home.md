Inspired by the house’s signature motifs; from bold geometrics and flowing
organic forms to woven effects and nature-inspired patterns; the collections
explore texture, colour and movement through sophisticated printing techniques
and innovative materials.

The result is wallpaper with the spirit of Missoni textiles; expressive,
contemporary and unmistakably Italian, bringing warmth, personality and a sense
of couture craftsmanship to interiors.
