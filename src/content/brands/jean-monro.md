  Inspired by childhood visits to England’s finest country houses with her
  decorator mother, Geraldine Monro, Jean Monro developed a passion for
  preserving historic textiles. This fascination led her to establish her
  textile house in 1982, recreating forgotten chintzes, faded fabrics and
  antique prints to ensure these treasures of English decoration lived on.
---

Today, Jean Monro is celebrated for authentic English florals, graceful stripes,
archival designs and decorative wallcoverings, many produced using traditional
techniques. Signature patterns such as Hollyhock are hand block printed using
146 individual blocks per repeat; a painstaking process that captures the depth,
texture and craftsmanship of the originals.

Collaborations such as the collection with renowned designer Nicholas Haslam
bring a fresh perspective to this heritage, resulting in textiles and
wallcoverings with true English character and timeless appeal.
